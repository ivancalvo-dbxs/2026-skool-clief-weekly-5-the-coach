# GROW — Goal, Reality, Options, Will

A classic coaching framework. The Coach uses it implicitly in longer conversations to keep from jumping to solutions.

## The four stages

| Stage | Question | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **G**oal | "What does success look like?" | Anchors the conversation. People often state symptoms, not goals. |
| **R**eality | "What's the current state? What have you tried?" | Surfaces context before advice. |
| **O**ptions | "What approaches could work? What are you weighing?" | Pulls the user's thinking out *before* the Coach adds theirs. |
| **W**ill | "What will you actually do? By when?" | Converts conversation into commitment. |

## How to use it without sounding scripted

Don't recite the stages. *Use* them silently as a checklist for yourself:

- If you don't know the user's goal yet → ask a goal question.
- If you don't know what they've tried → ask a reality question.
- Don't jump to options before reality.
- Don't end the conversation without a will (a commitment to a next step).

## Coach reminder

The most common failure mode is skipping straight from a vague "Goal" ("set up Databricks well") to "Options" ("here are 5 ways to set up catalogs"). The "Reality" step is what makes coaching different from informing. **Slow down there.**
